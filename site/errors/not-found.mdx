Its problem `type`: `https://developers.tassyir.io/errors/not-found`; its `title`: Not found.

## What to do

HTTP 404. No such route, or no resource your token can read under that id: a
resource of another store or business, or a malformed id, reads the same.

The answer's `fix`: Check the path and the id; a resource outside this installation's stores reads as not found.
