## What to do

HTTP 400. Your installation's version does not declare that key for that owner
type. Declare it under `[[data.definitions]]`, upload, and have the merchant
accept the update.

The answer's `fix`: Declare the key for this owner under [[data.definitions]] in the app's manifest; it can be written once the merchant accepts that version.
