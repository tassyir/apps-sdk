Its problem `type`: `https://developers.tassyir.io/errors/order-limit-reached`; its `title`: Order waiting for the plan allowance.

## What to do

HTTP 403. The order is waiting: it is past the plan's order allowance, and
cannot be confirmed until it unlocks (`orders/updated` with `waiting`).

The answer's `fix`: Retry after the merchant upgrades or the plan's next order cycle starts.
