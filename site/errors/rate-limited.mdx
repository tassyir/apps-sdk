## What to do

HTTP 429. Your installation's bucket is empty. Wait `Retry-After` seconds, then
retry; spread your calls using the `RateLimit-*` headers.

The answer's `fix`: Wait the number of seconds in Retry-After, then retry; keep an eye on RateLimit-Remaining to spread requests.
