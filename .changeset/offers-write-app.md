Offers, write side (scope `write_offers`): `admin.post('/offers')`, `admin.patch('/offers/{id}')`, `admin.delete('/offers/{id}')`, `/publish`, `/pause` and `/resume`, the codes routes (including `/codes/bulk`) and `admin.paginate` over `/offers/{id}/codes` and `/offers/{id}/redemptions` are typed. New exports: `addOfferCodes` (adds any number of codes, 250 per call, and gathers the answers) with `OFFER_CODES_BULK_MAX`, the types `OfferKind`, `CreateOfferInput`, `UpdateOfferInput`, `OfferTargetInput`, `OfferCode`, `OfferCodeInput`, `OfferCodesBulkResult`, `OfferRedemption` and `OfferRemoval`, and the declarative rule of an app-owned offer: `AppOfferConfig`, `AppOfferRule`, `AppOfferCondition`, `AppOfferLeaf`, `AppOfferAction`, `AppOfferClass`, `AppOfferChannel`, `AppOfferIds` and `APP_OFFER_RULE_LIMITS`. `POST /orders` takes `codes` and answers `rejectedCodes`, so `ResponseBody<'get', '/orders/{id}'>` now carries an optional `rejectedCodes` next to `data`. The `config`, `label`, `combinesWith` and `eligibility` of an offer body are free-form objects, no longer `Record<string, never>`. New problem codes, typed per operation: `OFFER_NOT_OWNED`, `OFFER_INVALID`, `OFFER_KIND_NOT_AVAILABLE`, `OFFER_KIND_IMMUTABLE`, `OFFER_TARGET_NOT_FOUND`, `OFFER_TARGET_OUTSIDE_STORE`, `OFFER_CODE_INVALID`, `OFFER_CODE_TAKEN`, `OFFER_CODE_NOT_FOUND`, `OFFER_STORE_NOT_FOUND`, `OFFER_VERSION_CONFLICT`, `OFFER_ARCHIVED`, `OFFER_NOT_PUBLISHED`, `OFFER_ENDED`, `OFFER_NOT_CODE_METHOD` and `OFFER_LIMIT_REACHED`. The Admin API types follow the API's OpenAPI document at backend `73c2d12b`.
