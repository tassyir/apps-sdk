The marketplace commands. `tassyir validate` and `tassyir deploy` read each `[[storefront.blocks]]` template and stylesheet (paths from the manifest's folder, never outside it) and hold them to `@tassyir/storefront-blocks`' `validateBlock`, the API's upload check, with every problem at once as `BLOCKS_INVALID` (exit 4), each at `file:line:column` with a fix, and the version's 100 KB of block files; `deploy` uploads them with the version: as `assets` (`{ files: [{ path, content }] }`) for an app on your own server, as text modules of the bundle for a hosted app (`BLOCK_ASSETS_NOT_ACCEPTED` when the API does not take them yet). `tassyir block preview [handle]` renders one block offline with sample data (`--lang`, `--settings`, `--data`; `--out` writes a page where no script can run). `tassyir listing pull | push` keeps the draft marketplace listing in `tassyir.listing.json`, with what it still needs. `tassyir submit --distribution public|unlisted [--note]` (no longer `COMMAND_NOT_AVAILABLE`) refuses an incomplete draft (`LISTING_INCOMPLETE`), submits, and follows the review's automated checklist to its verdict (exit 0 waiting for a person, 1 `SUBMISSION_CHECKS_FAILED` with each check's fix, 5 `SUBMISSION_CHECKING`); `--checks-only` runs the checklist as a dry run (`REVIEW_CHECKS_FAILED`). `tassyir submissions [id] [--follow]` shows the reviews. The developer-API client gains `getListing`, `putListing`, `submit`, `listSubmissions`, `getSubmission`, `runReviewChecks`, `getReviewChecks` and the upload's `assets`; its types follow the API's new OpenAPI document; `checkBlocks` and the review labels are exported.
